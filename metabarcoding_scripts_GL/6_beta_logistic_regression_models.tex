The best fitting model is the null model, which means that the
proportion of marine prey is constant across all predators and distances
from the coastline. However, the model that is second best fit with a
small delta BIC is the model that only takes into account the predator
species. In this model, feeding behavior or marine prey differs among
predators, but is independent of the location relative to the coastline.
